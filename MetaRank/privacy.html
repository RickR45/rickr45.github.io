Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

MetaRank ("we", "us") is an app for ranking and sharing the movies, TV, albums, songs, books, restaurants and places you love. This policy explains what we collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), username and display name.
  • Profile — a profile photo and bio, if you add them.
  • What you post — ratings, rankings, reviews and review photos, comments, likes, bookmarks, favourites, the people you follow, people you tag or mention, and messages you send in direct or group chats.
  • Location — only if you allow it, and only while you use the app. Your device location is used to find restaurants and places near you. We do not store your location history.
  • Photos you choose to import — when you use "import from photo", the image is sent for processing (see AI features below) and is not stored by us.
  • Purchases — whether you have MetaRank Premium, when it renews or expires, and the group-suggestion credits you have bought and used. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your card or billing details.
  • Notifications — a push token for your device, if you allow notifications.
  • Usage needed to run the service — counters for your daily AI allowance, and any reports you file or accounts you block.

How we use it

  • To run MetaRank: your rankings and lists, your profile, the feed of people you follow, comments, messages and notifications.
  • To make recommendations and match scores — only if you turn on AI features (below).
  • To provide Premium and group-suggestion credits you have bought.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, and removing content or accounts that break our Terms of Use.

What other people can see

Your username, display name, profile photo, bio, rankings, ratings, reviews, comments, favourites, and who you follow are visible to other MetaRank users. Messages are visible only to the people in that conversation. People you block cannot see your content in their feed, cannot follow you, and cannot comment on your rankings.

AI features and Google Gemini

For You picks, match scores, group suggestions, taste match, the "Your taste" write-up and photo import are generated with Google Gemini, a third-party AI service. We only send data to Gemini if you allow it — we ask when you sign up (or the first time you open this version of the app), and you can change your answer at any time in Profile → Account & Privacy.

When AI features are on, we send Google the titles and scores you have rated; for group suggestions, the usernames and ratings of the group's members; and for photo import, the photo you choose. We never send your email address or password. Google processes this data under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. If AI features are off, everything else in MetaRank still works.

Service providers we share data with

  • Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States) — our database, authentication, file storage and server functions. Holds everything described above. Privacy policy
  • Google Gemini — AI features, only with your permission, as described above.
  • Google Places — restaurant search. Receives your search text and, if you allow location, your approximate position. Privacy policy
  • Apple and RevenueCat — in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives your MetaRank account ID and your App Store purchase records so we can confirm what you have bought. RevenueCat privacy policy
  • Expo — delivers push notifications. Receives your device's push token and the notification text. Privacy policy
  • Content databases — The Movie Database (TMDB), Open Library, MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive, Apple's music charts, Wikipedia, and OpenStreetMap (Nominatim). The app looks titles and places up in these services; they receive the search terms, not your account information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. Content you delete is removed straight away. When you delete your account, everything listed under "What we collect" is permanently deleted, including your photos and your purchase records with RevenueCat; residual copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten on their normal rotation.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in Profile → Account & Privacy → Delete account. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel that in your Apple ID's subscription settings.
  • AI features — turn them on or off in Profile → Account & Privacy.
  • Location and notifications — allow or revoke them in your iPhone's Settings at any time.
  • Block or report anyone from their profile, or by long-pressing something they wrote.
  • Access, correct or export your data — edit your profile in the app, or email us and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights under local law; contact us to exercise them.

Children

MetaRank is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to it is restricted by per-user database rules so each account can change only its own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will announce significant changes in the app before they take effect.
